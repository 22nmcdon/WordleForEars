How this works

    The same three colours, everywhere

    • Sage — that is it. What you named is what was played.
    • Gold — close. The next band along, a semitone out, the right feel and the wrong figure. What "close" means is the mode's own business, and the second column spells it out.
    • Rust — not close. Never a mark against you: it is how far the two things sit apart.
    • Seven modes, three tiers each. Every one has its own daily — the same puzzle for everybody, seeded from the date — and unlimited practice, which runs in a cooler light so you always know which you are in.
    • Everything is kept in this browser. No account, no server.
