How to play

Press Play chord and listen. Pick the root and the quality you think you heard, then submit. You get six guesses.

  • Green — exactly right.
  • Yellow — close: the note you picked is in the chord but isn't the root, or the quality you picked shares structure with the answer.
  • Grey — not in the chord at all.

The third column scores your whole guess: how many of the answer's notes your chord actually contains.

Everything is stored locally in your browser. No account, no server.
