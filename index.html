Chord practice
How this works

A chord is played. You name what kind of chord it is — and each guess tells you how close what you named sits to what you heard.

  • Play the chord, then name its quality. Arpeggiate spreads it out one note at a time; Reference C gives you middle C to orient by. Play it as many times as you like.
  • The root is not part of it. Every puzzle is rooted somewhere different, so there is nothing to anchor on. Naming the root by ear is absolute pitch — a different skill, and its own mode later.
  • Sage — that is the chord. The quality you named is the one being played.
  • Gold — some of it is right. What you named shares a note above the root with what you heard: the right third and the wrong seventh, say.
  • Rust — nothing in common. Not a mark against you: it is how far the two chords sit apart.
  • The second column counts the notes. 2/3 means your chord has two of the three notes above the root that the answer has — which is what narrows the next guess.
  • Three guesses on Easy, four further up. Enough to work it out, not enough to press every button. The daily is the same chord for everyone; practice is unlimited and runs in a cooler light.
  • Everything is kept in this browser. No account, no server.
